What a CoA actually reports

A Certificate of Analysis is batch documentation for laboratory material — identity, purity, and related checks. It is not a clinical result, a dose guide, or a product claim for human use.

Science pages cover programs, platforms, and documentation. Shop listings remain research chemicals only.
